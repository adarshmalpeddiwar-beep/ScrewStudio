STEP files are AP214 faceted B-rep solids in millimetres, which SolidWorks, Fusion, Onshape, FreeCAD and Inventor import as closed solid bodies. The origin sits on the screw axis at the length reference plane: under the head, or on the top face for countersunk heads. +Z points toward the head.
